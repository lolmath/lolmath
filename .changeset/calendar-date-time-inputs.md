Add `Calendar`, `RangeCalendar`, `DateField`, `TimeField`, `DatePicker`, and `DateRangePicker`. Selected dates get a continuous gold border with a hextech-black fill, rounded at the true start/end of a selection and at the start/end of every row it wraps through. `Calendar`/`RangeCalendar` support a `header="picker"` mode with Select-driven month and year dropdowns.
