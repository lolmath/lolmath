Scaffold the remaining League of Legends mechanics as stubs. Sixteen new
modules cover critical strikes, vamp, healing and shielding, crowd control,
damage, gold, experience, regeneration, range, cast times, death timers,
minions, the jungle, objectives, vision and scoreboard statistics, and the
attack speed, ability haste, movement speed and stacking modules were extended.
Every new function is declared with its final signature and documentation, and
throws until it is implemented.
